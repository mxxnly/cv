This Policy explains how the website {{site.domain}} ({{brand.name}}, {{seller.legal_name}}, a sole proprietor registered under the laws of Ukraine) uses cookies and similar technologies (including localStorage) and how you manage your consent.

## 1. What Cookies Are

1.1. Cookies are small text files that a site stores in your browser. localStorage is browser storage in which the site keeps settings without sending them to the server.

## 2. Which Categories We Use

2.1. **Necessary.** Work without consent because they are required for the site to function correctly: the selected theme, the interface language and your choice in the cookie banner. Stored in your browser's localStorage.

2.2. **Analytics.** Google Analytics 4 — visit statistics (which pages are viewed, where visitors come from). Enabled only after your consent.

2.3. **Advertising.** Google Ads — conversion tracking (request, proceeding to payment, payment) to measure advertising effectiveness. Enabled only after your consent.

## 3. List of Cookies and Records

| Name | Category | Purpose | Duration |

| `theme` | necessary | light or dark theme | until deleted |

| `_ga` | analytics | Google Analytics visitor identifier | 2 years |
| `_ga_*` | analytics | Google Analytics session | 2 years |
| `_gcl_au` | advertising | Google Ads conversion tracking | 3 months |

## 4. Consent and Google Consent Mode v2

4.1. On your first visit we show a banner with the buttons "Accept", "Reject" and "Settings". Analytics and advertising do not start until you have given consent.

4.2. We use Google Consent Mode v2: until consent is obtained, all signals for Google (analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization) are set to "denied".

4.3. Declining analytics and advertising does not limit the operation of the site.

## 5. How to Change or Withdraw Consent

5.1. Click "Cookie settings" in the site footer and change your choice. Changes take effect immediately.

5.2. You may also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. After that, the banner will appear again.

## 6. Third-Party Services

6.1. Analytics and advertising data is processed by Google (Google Ireland Limited for users in Europe). See Google's privacy policy and Google's description of technologies for partner sites for details.
